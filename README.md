## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

In this system, multiple farmers share three vans to transport their vegetables to a common selling location. First, they sell vegetables in bulk to nearby shops within a selected radius. After completing shop orders, they sell the remaining vegetables directly to customers.

The platform manages farmer registration, vegetable details, shop orders, van booking and sales records.

How it solves the problem:

- Farmers share transportation expenses.
- Farmers get direct access to multiple shops.
- Customers can buy vegetables at potentially lower prices.


Innovation and Uniqueness:

1. Three shared vans reduce individual transport costs.
2. Shops receive first priority for bulk purchases.
3. Remaining vegetables are sold directly to customers.

Implementation steps:
Create farmer and shop registration.
Allow farmers to list vegetables and prices.
Develop a shared van booking system.
Collect advance orders from shops.
Update stock after shop sales.
Notify customers about available vegetables.
Calculate each farmer's sales and transport expenses.

## Feasibility and Viability
The project can be started using a simple web application and three rented or shared vans. Farmers and shopkeepers can register and place orders through the platform.

Challenges:

- Availability of vans.
- Differences in selling prices.
- Fuel and transportation costs.


Solutions:

- Partner with local vehicle owners.
- Maintain transparent prices.
- Share transport costs among farmers.
## Impact and Benefits
For Farmers:

- Better selling opportunities.
- Reduced transportation expenses.
- Direct access to shops and customers.
- Improved chances of selling their produce. 
For Shopkeepers and Customers:

- Direct access to fresh vegetables.
- Potentially lower purchase prices.
- Better price transparency.
- convenient availability of vegetables.
